    Seven stages, in the order to play them. Story first, then strangers, collectibles and activities, then robberies and money, and 100% completion last — the platinum pops with the final trophy. Before every chapter, make a manual save: there is no mission replay, and the one missable trophy, Positive Mental Attitude, has a Chapter 1 meditation spot that is reported to disappear after the chapter.

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy in this list can be missed. Everything else either unlocks through the story or can be done in free-roam after it, because PowerPyx reports there is no mission replay but free-roam continues after the story. The advice below is what protects a run.

    How not to miss it

    1. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter. If a spot is missed, a manual save is the only way back — there is no mission replay.
    2. Meditate the moment a spot is offered. Do not walk past a meditation spot meaning to come back later.
    3. Finish Chapter 1's spot before the chapter ends. The spot at Jason's house is reported to disappear after Chapter 1.

    About the list

    Grand Theft Auto VI launches 19 November 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar, Sony and Microsoft had published no official trophy list before launch, and the PSNProfiles community called the circulating 55-trophy list "probably fake". This guide uses that list exactly as the kit carries it — 55 trophies, 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze, 28 of them hidden on PlayStation until earned — and each guide marks steps beyond the official description as reported or unverified. Check the list again after launch; the names, descriptions and grades will be corrected if the real list differs.

    How to use this tracker

    1. Tick a trophy's box when you earn it — your progress is saved in this browser automatically.
    2. For count trophies, open the trophy and use the count tracker to log how far you have got (for example 87 of 250 stunts).
    3. Use Export progress to save a backup file, and Import progress to restore it on another browser or device.
    4. Turn on "Hide story spoilers" if you have not finished the story: story trophies stay hidden until you earn them.

    This guide is a set of static files. It works with the network off and sends nothing anywhere: the only request it makes is for its own data file, app/data/trophies.json. Links to sources open only when you follow them.
