      About this list — read before you trust it

      GTA VI launches 19 November 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, set in the state of Leonida around Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos (Rockstar Games). Rockstar, Sony and Microsoft had published no official trophy list before launch, and the PSNProfiles community called the circulating 55-trophy list “probably fake”. This guide uses that list exactly as the kit carries it — 55 trophies, 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze, 28 hidden — and says so in each guide where a step goes beyond the official description: reported where a guide reports it, unverified where no source we could check confirms it. Check the list again after launch; the names, descriptions and grades here will be corrected if the real list differs.
